MG Live（以下简称"我们"）非常重视用户个人信息和隐私保护。在您使用 MG Live APP 及相关服务时，我们可能会收集和使用您的相关信息。我们希望通过本《隐私政策》向您说明，在您使用我们的服务时，我们如何收集、使用、储存、共享和转让这些信息，以及我们为您提供的访问、更新、删除和保护这些信息的方式。

一、我们如何收集和使用您的个人信息

1. 账号注册与登录：当您注册 MG Live 账号时，我们会收集您的手机号码、验证码，用于创建账号、登录验证与找回密码。

2. 车辆绑定与服务：当您绑定车辆时，我们会收集车辆识别代号（VIN）、车牌号、车型信息，用于提供车辆状态查询、远程控制、保养提醒等服务。

3. 位置信息：当您使用附近经销商、道路救援、行程记录等功能时，我们会收集您的地理位置信息。您可以在系统设置中随时关闭定位权限，关闭后不影响其他功能的使用。

4. 设备信息：为保障服务正常运行、预防恶意程序，我们会收集设备型号、操作系统版本、设备标识符、网络状态。

二、我们如何使用 Cookie 和同类技术

为确保 APP 正常运转、为您获得更轻松的访问体验，我们会在您的设备上存储名为 Cookie 的小数据文件。

三、我们如何共享、转让、公开披露您的个人信息

我们不会与任何公司、组织和个人共享您的个人信息，但以下情况除外：事先获得您明确的同意或授权；根据适用的法律法规、法律程序的要求、强制性的行政或司法要求所必需的情况。

四、我们如何保护您的个人信息

我们已使用符合业界标准的安全防护措施保护您提供的个人信息，防止数据遭到未经授权的访问、公开披露、使用、修改、损坏或丢失。

五、您的权利

您可以通过 APP「我的 - 设置」访问、更正、删除您的个人信息，也可以注销账号。如您无法通过上述方式行使权利，可通过本政策末尾的联系方式与我们取得联系。

六、我们如何处理未成年人的个人信息

我们的产品、网站和服务主要面向成人。如果没有父母或监护人的同意，未成年人不应创建自己的个人信息主体账户。

七、本政策如何更新

我们可能适时修订本政策的条款。未经您明确同意，我们不会削减您按照本政策所应享有的权利。对于重大变更，我们会通过弹窗提示等显著方式通知您。

八、如何联系我们

如您对本政策有任何疑问、意见或建议，可通过客服电话 400-828-1088 与我们联系。

MG Live Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 15, 2026

Effective Date: October 15, 2026

MG Live (hereinafter referred to as "we") attaches great importance to the protection of your personal information and privacy. When you use the MG Live APP and related services, we may collect and use your relevant information. Through this Privacy Policy, we want to explain how we collect, use, store, share, and transfer this information when you use our services, as well as how we provide you with access, update, deletion, and protection of this information.

1. How We Collect and Use Your Personal Information

1.1 Account Registration and Login: When you register an MG Live account, we collect your mobile number and verification code to create your account, verify your login, and recover your password.

1.2 Vehicle Binding and Services: When you bind a vehicle, we collect the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN), license plate number, and vehicle model to provide vehicle status queries, remote control, and maintenance reminders.

1.3 Location Information: When you use features such as nearby dealers, roadside assistance, and trip records, we collect your geographic location. You may disable location permissions in your system settings at any time without affecting other functions.

1.4 Device Information: To ensure proper service operation and prevent malicious programs, we collect device model, operating system version, device identifier, and network status.

2. How We Use Cookies and Similar Technologies

To ensure the APP functions properly and to provide you with a smoother access experience, we store small data files called Cookies on your device.

3. How We Share, Transfer, and Disclose Your Personal Information

We do not share your personal information with any company, organization, or individual, except in the following cases: with your prior explicit consent or authorization; or as required by applicable laws, regulations, legal procedures, mandatory administrative or judicial requirements.

4. How We Protect Your Personal Information

We have implemented security measures that meet industry standards to protect the personal information you provide against unauthorized access, public disclosure, use, modification, damage, or loss.

5. Your Rights

You may access, correct, or delete your personal information, or cancel your account, via "My - Settings" in the APP. If you are unable to exercise these rights through the above means, please contact us using the information at the end of this policy.

6. How We Handle Minors' Personal Information

Our products, websites, and services are primarily intended for adults. Without the consent of a parent or guardian, minors should not create their own personal information subject accounts.

7. How This Policy Is Updated

We may revise the terms of this policy from time to time. Without your explicit consent, we will not reduce the rights you are entitled to under this policy. For material changes, we will notify you through prominent means such as popup prompts.

8. How to Contact Us

If you have any questions, comments, or suggestions regarding this policy, please contact us via customer service hotline 400-828-1088.
